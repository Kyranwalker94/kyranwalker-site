Our Allotment 🥔

Taking on a family allotment at the end of 2026, clearing and covering the plot over winter with big plans to get it fully productive in 2027.

The project

Towards the end of 2026 we inherited an allotment plot that's been in the family for as long as I've been alive, previously kept going by relatives and now passed on to the next generation. We're sharing it three ways with my Dad and my Aunt, splitting the watering, weeding, and general upkeep on a rota between us.

It's come to us a little late in the season to get much going before winter, so the priority for now has been clearing what was there and getting the ground ready, with the real growing starting properly in 2027. This page will track progress as that gets underway.

The plan

  • Full assessment of the existing plot, beds, and any structures worth keeping
  • Clearance of overgrown areas ready for winter
  • Covering beds over winter to protect and prepare the soil
  • Shared rota with my Dad and Aunt for watering, weeding, and general maintenance
  • Full grow list planning for the 2027 season once the ground is ready

Over winter

With the plot cleared and covered, there isn't much happening at the allotment itself until spring. The onions, garlic, and broad beans we'd normally want going in now are instead being grown at home over winter (see Our Garden), with the allotment left resting ready for a proper first season in 2027.
